Features

Built for accounting practices,
not adapted from a field-service app

Every feature is designed around internal cost visibility — not GPS punch-in, not client billing.

⏰

Weekly Time Log

Staff enter start and end times per client and service. Duration is auto-calculated, rounded to the nearest quarter-hour. No double-entry, no spreadsheets.

📈

Capacity & Cost Analytics

See total cost by employee, client, and service type. Drill down from team to individual or from client to task. Know at a glance where your practice spends its hours.

✅

Timesheet Approval Workflow

Staff submit their full week for manager review. Managers approve or reject with one click. Approved timesheets are locked — entries can't be edited retroactively without re-opening.

👥

Team & Client Management

Set an hourly cost rate per staff member. Manage your client list and service types directly in the app. Invite team members via a shareable link — no IT setup.

📤

CSV & PDF Export

Export cost reports by employee, client, or service for any date range. Download as a formatted PDF or raw CSV for your own tools — no data lock-in.

🔒

Manager & Staff Roles

Managers see the full team view and can approve, reject, or edit any timesheet. Staff see only their own entries. Role separation is enforced at the database level.

Cost Visibility

Internal cost — not client billing

Timeki Practice answers one question: what does it cost your practice to serve each client? This is your internal staff cost — not what you bill.

  • Cost = hours × staff hourly cost rate (set per employee)
  • Drill-down views: Employee → Client → Service • Client → Service → Employee • Service → Employee → Client
  • Filter by week, month, financial year, or custom date range
  • 6 KPI cards: total hours, total cost, active clients, active staff, avg cost/client, avg hrs/staff
